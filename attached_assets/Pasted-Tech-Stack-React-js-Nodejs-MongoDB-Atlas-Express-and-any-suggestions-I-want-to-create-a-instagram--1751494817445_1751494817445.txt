\= SPEC-1: iShopp Mobile Application
\:sectnums:
\:toc:

\== Background

The iShopp mobile application is conceived as a community‑driven platform that turns everyday shoppers into real‑time contributors of in‑store specials, price drops, and pop‑up promotions. Traditional printed catalogues and scattered online ads make it difficult for consumers to know which retailer has the best deal at any given moment. By enabling users to **snap**, **scan**, and **share** instant offers as they encounter them, iShopp bridges this information gap and helps households stretch their budgets.

Smartphone penetration, high mobile‑data coverage, and rising food and fuel costs mean that price‑sensitive consumers constantly look for ways to coordinate trips and reduce spend. iShopp leverages these trends by:

* Crowdsourcing geo‑tagged promotions that expire quickly (e.g., same‑day bakery markdowns, limited‑stock clearance deals).
* Aggregating retailer flyers and official APIs where available to compare against user‑generated prices.
* Offering collaborative shopping lists so families and friends can split tasks, avoid duplicate purchases, and jointly unlock bulk‑buy savings.
* Introducing an in‑app **token** system that gamifies contributions (earn tokens for verified uploads) and enables micro‑rewards or cash‑back redemptions with partner brands.

The ultimate business goal is to build a data pipeline of hyper‑local pricing intelligence that can be monetised through brand promotions, anonymous market insights, and affiliate revenue, while giving end‑users a compelling reason to return daily.

\== Background

The iShopp mobile application is conceived as a community‑driven platform that turns everyday shoppers into real‑time contributors of in‑store specials, price drops, and pop‑up promotions. Traditional printed catalogues and scattered online ads make it difficult for consumers to know which retailer has the best deal at any given moment. By enabling users to **snap**, **scan**, and **share** instant offers as they encounter them, iShopp bridges this information gap and helps households stretch their budgets.

Smartphone penetration, high mobile‑data coverage, and rising food and fuel costs mean that price‑sensitive consumers constantly look for ways to coordinate trips and reduce spend. iShopp leverages these trends by:

* Crowdsourcing geo‑tagged promotions that expire quickly (e.g., same‑day bakery markdowns, limited‑stock clearance deals).
* Aggregating retailer flyers and official APIs where available to compare against user‑generated prices.
* Offering collaborative shopping lists so families and friends can split tasks, avoid duplicate purchases, and jointly unlock bulk‑buy savings.
* Introducing an in‑app **token** system that gamifies contributions (earn tokens for verified uploads) and enables micro‑rewards or cash‑back redemptions with partner brands.

The ultimate business goal is to build a data pipeline of hyper‑local pricing intelligence that can be monetised through brand promotions, anonymous market insights, and affiliate revenue, while giving end‑users a compelling reason to return daily.

\== Requirements

The following requirements are categorized using the **MoSCoW** framework.

\=== Must Have

* **User onboarding (email, mobile, or social login)**
* **Snap‑Scan‑Share workflow** – capture photo, auto‑crop, OCR price & brand, add manual metadata.
* **Geo‑tagging & time‑stamping** for every special.
* **Real‑time feed & search** filtered by distance, store, category, keyword.
* **Collaborative shopping list** (create, share, edit in real time).
* **In‑app messenger** contextual to a special and to shopping‑list groups.
* **Token wallet & payment gateway** – send/receive **iShopp ZAR‑pegged tokens**, pay participating South‑African retailers, and settle to fiat (PayShap or EFT) for merchants.
* **Store‑locator map** showing specials near you with deep‑links to navigation apps.
* **Basic content moderation & abuse reporting** (image review queue, profanity filter).
* **Admin dashboard (web)** for brand partners to upload official offers and redeem tokens.
* **Analytics & logging** for usage, error, and promotion engagement.

\=== Should Have

* **Barcode/QR scan** to identify product and auto‑fill description.
* **Push notifications** for nearby or subscribed‑brand specials.
* **Gamified rewards** – award bonus tokens for first uploader, verified price match, etc.
* **Offer‑verification workflow** (crowd‑votes + moderator override).
* **Offline capture & later sync** when connectivity is restored.
* **Dark mode** and basic accessibility support.
* * International currency support beyond ZAR.
* On‑device machine‑learning for object detection.
* Multi‑language localisation beyond English 
* **Retailer POS integration** for automatic token redemption.
* **Dynamic pricing‑insights dashboard** sold to FMCG brands.
* **Augmented‑reality (AR) aisle view** overlaying specials.
* **Social share to WhatsApp/Facebook** with deep‑link back to app.

Vision & Problem

* Rising cost of groceries → shoppers hunt deals.
* Information is fragmented across flyers & word‑of‑mouth.
* **iShopp** crowdsources real‑time in‑store specials, helping South‑African families **save time & money**.



Deliver a production app that lets users:

. Snap‑Scan‑Share verified specials.
. Search a geo‑feed of offers.
. Maintain collaborative shopping lists.
. Transfer & spend **ITK tokens** (ZAR‑pegged) via a central ledger.


 Must‑Have Features

* Auth (email/SMS) & profiles.
* Offer capture with OCR price parse.
* Real‑time feed & search filters.
* Token wallet, PayShap/EFT merchant payouts.
* In‑app chat & list collaboration.
* Map‑based store locator.
* Basic moderation & analytics.
